**Settings → Devices** sets the sound devices by function, so the ringtone can ring somewhere other than where you talk. You can switch a device in the middle of a call.

![Settings → Devices](https://ai-softphone.com/screenshots/macos/en/light/devices.png)

## Microphone

- The drop-down chooses the device; **System default** follows what macOS uses.
- The bar under it moves when the microphone hears you — say something to check that it is the right one (*Say something — this moves when the microphone hears you*).
- **Volume** sets the microphone volume.

## Speakers

- The drop-down chooses the device you hear the other side on; **System default** follows macOS.
- **Test** plays a sound through it.
- **Volume** is the loudness of the other side.

## Ringtone

- The first drop-down chooses the melody (for example **Classic**). The folder button next to it lets you pick your own sound file.
- **Test** plays it.
- The second drop-down chooses where the ringtone plays. **Same as the speakers** keeps it in one place; choose another device to ring, for example, on a loudspeaker while you wear a headset.
- **Volume** is the loudness of the ringtone.

## Other sounds

**Volume** of the program's other sounds — the ones that are not a ringtone or a voice.
