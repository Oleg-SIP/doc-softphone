The main window is the phone itself. It changes with what is happening: the dialler when nothing is, the call card during a call, a list when there are several calls, and a recording screen while a meeting is being captured.

## The dialler

![The dialler](https://ai-softphone.com/screenshots/macos/en/light/dialer.png)

- **Number** — type or paste the number to call. The **×** at the right end of the field clears it.
- The round keys **1–9**, **\***, **0** and **#** have the letters of a phone keypad under them. They fill in the number, and during a call they send tones (DTMF).
- The green handset key places the call.

## A call in progress

![A call in progress](https://ai-softphone.com/screenshots/macos/en/light/call.png)

While a call is going on, the number field moves to the top with a keypad icon inside it, and the call is shown on a card:

- the state and the length of the call (**On a call · 0:21**), the name of the other party, **Line** and the name of the account the call is on, and the number;
- two vertical level bars at the sides of the card, one for each channel of the sound;
- a row of buttons: record (circle), mute (microphone), hold (pause) and the red **hang up** button;
- a second row: transfer (handset with an arrow) and the keypad.

If the number is known in **Contacts**, its name is shown instead of the number.

## Several calls at once

![Several calls](https://ai-softphone.com/screenshots/macos/en/light/calls.png)

A new incoming call appears on its own card above the list, with a green, a yellow and a red button and a line saying who you are talking to now (**On a call with Maria Ellis**). The list below shows every call with its state — **On hold**, **On a call**, **Incoming call** — and the account it is on. A pause icon marks a call on hold and a speaker icon marks the one you are talking on.

## Conference

![A conference](https://ai-softphone.com/screenshots/macos/en/light/conference.png)

Calls that have been joined show as one **Conference** card on the account's line. Each participant is listed with the time in the call and their own **Hang up** button. The buttons below record, mute and end the conference for everybody; the wide button at the bottom splits the conference back into separate calls.

## The bar at the bottom

| Element | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Account chips (for example **Marlow Software**, **Support desk**) | One for every account. The green dot means the account is registered on the PBX. The highlighted chip is the account the next call is placed from. |
| **DND** | Do not disturb. |
| **Capture · ready** and **Record** | The state of [external recording](../external-recording/setup.md) and the button that starts it by hand. |
| **Contacts**, **History** | Open [Contacts and calls](contacts-history.md). |
| **Recordings** | Opens the [Recordings window](../recordings/recordings-window.md). |
| **Settings** | Opens the [settings](settings-overview.md). |

The program can also live in the tray (menu bar) and be brought up with a hotkey.
