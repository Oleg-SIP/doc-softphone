AI Softphone connects to your phone system the way a desk phone does: it registers
on an IP PBX, or with a VoIP provider, as a SIP account. Once the account is
registered, the phone places and takes calls on that line.

## What you need

Ask your PBX administrator or your VoIP provider for a SIP account — usually an
extension. Three details of it are enough:

| Detail | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Username** | The extension number or login of the account | `204` |
| **Password** | The SIP password of that account — not the password of the PBX's web interface | |
| **Server address** | The domain name or IP address of the PBX or the provider | `pbx.example.com` |

<Aside>
If you were also given an authentication name, a port, a transport (UDP, TCP or
TLS) or an outbound proxy, enter the three details above first, and the rest under
**Server settings** — see [Advanced SIP account settings](../advanced/).
</Aside>

## Adding an account

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** — at the bottom of the main window — and choose **Accounts**.

2. Add a new account below the list of accounts.

3. Fill in the form:

   - **Name (optional)** — what the line is called in the account list and at the
     bottom of the main window, for example your company's name or "Support desk".
   - **Username**, **Password** and **Server address** — the three details from the
     table above.

4. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

The password is never written to a file: it is kept in your operating system's
keyring, the secure password store the system itself uses.

## Checking that the line works

After you save, the phone registers on the server. A registered account has a
**green dot** beside its name — in the account list, and at the bottom of the main
window, where all your lines are shown.

<Screenshot
  name="dialer.png"
  alt="The main window: the keypad, and the registered lines with green dots at the bottom"
/>

To be sure, call another extension: type its number and press the green button.

## Managing accounts

You can add as many accounts as you have, each with its own settings. In the
account list:

- **The checkbox** beside an account switches the line on or off without deleting
  it. A switched-off account has a grey dot and says **Not used**.
- **Disconnect** unregisters the line from the server.
- **▲** and **▼** move the account up or down the list.
- **Delete**, in the account's form, removes the account altogether.
- **Answer automatically**, under **Answering**, makes the line pick up incoming
  calls by itself — for example where the PBX expects the phone to answer on its
  own.

When several lines are registered, all of them are shown at the bottom of the main
window: choose the one the next call should go out on.

## If the dot does not turn green

<Steps>

1. Check the username, the password and the server address. A typo or the wrong
   password is the most common cause.

2. Open **Settings → Diagnostics** and the **SIP** tab. It shows every message
   between the phone and the server as it happens. Find the last `REGISTER` and
   the answer the server gave to it:

   | Answer | What it means |
   | --- | --- |
   | `200 OK` | The account is registered. |
   | `403 Forbidden`, or `401 Unauthorized` over and over | The server does not accept the username or the password. |
   | `404 Not Found` | The server does not know this username. |
   | No answer at all | The server cannot be reached at this address. Check the address, then the port and the transport under **Server settings**. A firewall or a VPN may also be in the way. |

3. If that does not settle it, show the **SIP** tab to your PBX administrator: it
   is the record they will ask for.

</Steps>
